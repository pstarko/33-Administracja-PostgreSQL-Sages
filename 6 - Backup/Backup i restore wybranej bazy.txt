Na poziomie terminala (user postgres)

1. pg_dump -Fc -d Company -f /backups/company.dump --backup bazy Company (Case Sensitive)

2. pg_dumpall --globals-only -f /backups/globals.sql --backup tablespace & ról


RESTORE

3. psql -f /backups/globals.sql -- odtworzenie ustawień tablespace & ról

4. pg_restore -C -d postgres /backups/company.dump -- restore bazy
